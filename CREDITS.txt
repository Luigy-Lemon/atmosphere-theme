Atmosphere theme for Clean Your TV (LG webOS edition).

Original wallpaper: generated for the owner's Atmosphere TV project, reused byte-for-byte from app/assets/wallpaper.png.
Theme styling: derived from Atmosphere TV's dark blue palette and Gallery dock.
Launcher, layout engine, controls and bundled Outfit font: supplied by Clean Your TV, https://github.com/Razdva122/clean-your-tv-lg-webos (MIT; font under its bundled OFL).
App artwork on the TV is supplied by the installed apps. The browser preview includes Clean Your TV's reference app artwork; those trademarks and images belong to their respective owners and are not licensed by this theme's MIT license.
